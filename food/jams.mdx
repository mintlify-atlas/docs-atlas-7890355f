Jams are preserved fruit and berry spreads stored in a craftable Jar. Most jams share the same base nutrition and saturation values, making them reliable mid-game food sources, but a few stand out with powerful or unusual effects. To make any jam you first need an empty Jar.

## Crafting the Jar

The **Jar** is a reusable container crafted from glass and planks at a crafting table. The advancement "For Jar Jar's Sake!" triggers the first time you obtain one.

Once you have a Jar, combine it with the appropriate fruit or berry in a crafting recipe to produce the filled jam item.

<Note>
  Jams are `ConsumableItem` instances — they are eaten directly from your hand, not placed. Unlike cheese wheels, jams do not produce a container item on consumption.
</Note>

## Special jams

Three jams have effects or mechanics that make them meaningfully different from the standard list.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Chorus Fruit Jam" icon="sparkles">
    **Nutrition:** 7 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.8

    Made from chorus fruit. When eaten, it teleports you to a random nearby location — identical behaviour to eating a raw chorus fruit. Useful for escaping danger or navigating the End, but be careful where you eat it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Golden Apple Jam" icon="star">
    **Nutrition:** 8 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.8 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Rarity:** Rare

    **Effects (100% chance):**
    - Regeneration II for 60 seconds
    - Absorption I for 120 seconds

    Made from a golden apple combined with a jar. This jam has **Rare** rarity (gold name in the tooltip), can be eaten even when your hunger bar is full (`alwaysEat`), and applies two powerful effects simultaneously. It is the most potent food item in the mod.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Golden Apple Jam can be eaten at any hunger level, making it useful as an emergency heal mid-combat even when you are not hungry.
</Tip>
