Feast dishes are the most substantial food items in Yeast 'n Feast. Every feast dish is served either in a bowl or on a plate, and eating one grants a custom 4-minute potion effect that goes well beyond ordinary food saturation. Bowl dishes grant **Overfed**, while plate dishes grant **Vigorous** — both last for 4 minutes (4:00).

For a full breakdown of what these effects do, see [Mob Effects](/mechanics/mob-effects).

## The Overfed effect

Bowl dishes apply **Overfed (4:00)** when eaten. Overfed prevents your saturation from decreasing for the duration, meaning food you eat while the effect is active does not drain away — you stay full. It also slightly reduces movement speed (−10%).

<Note>
  You can still eat while Overfed is active. The effect only prevents saturation loss, not food consumption.
</Note>

## The Vigorous effect

Plate dishes apply **Vigorous (4:00)** when eaten. Vigorous increases your maximum health by 3 hearts (+6 to the health attribute) and boosts healing received, making you noticeably more durable for the duration.

## Plate dishes

All plate dishes grant Vigorous for 4,800 ticks (4 minutes). They are crafted at a crafting table and return an empty **Bowl** when consumed.

    **Nutrition:** 11 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.8

    **Effects:** Vigorous (4:00) + Strength I (10s)

<Tip>
  Stuffed Rye Dumplings are the only feast dish that provides a secondary combat effect alongside its standard feast buff. The Strength I lasts 10 seconds, so eat them just before a fight for maximum benefit.
</Tip>

## Compat feast dishes

Additional feast dishes are available when compatible mods are installed. These follow the same bowl/plate rules and grant the same Overfed or Vigorous effects.

| Item | Mod required | Type | Nutrition | Saturation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garden Soup | Farmer's Delight | Bowl (Overfed) | 13 | 0.8 |
| Oatmeal with Elderberries | Farm and Charm | Bowl (Overfed) | 12 | 0.8 |
| Maple-Rye Pancakes | Farm and Charm | Plate (Vigorous) | 12 | 0.8 |
| Stuffed Mandrake | Hexalia | Plate (Vigorous) | 12 | 0.8 |

See [Mod Compatibility](/compat/overview) for setup instructions.
