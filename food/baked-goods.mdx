Yeast 'n Feast adds three artisan breads and seven baked goods to craft at a crafting table. Breads are hearty and filling, while baked goods range from fruity rolls and pies to rich tarts and savoury quiches — some granting bonus potion effects on consumption.

## Breads

All three breads share the same nutrition and saturation values and are crafted using their respective grain (barley or rye) or sweetener (molasses) alongside other crafting table ingredients.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Barley Bread" icon="wheat">
    **Nutrition:** 5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.8

    Crafted from barley flour and water. A filling staple for early-game survival.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rye Bread" icon="wheat">
    **Nutrition:** 5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.8

    Crafted from rye flour. A darker, denser loaf with identical stats to barley bread.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Molasses Bread" icon="wheat">
    **Nutrition:** 5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.8

    Crafted using molasses for a subtly sweet flavour. Same stats as the other breads.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Barley and rye must be grown from seeds, then harvested and processed into their respective grains before you can bake bread. See [Crops & Ingredients](/crops/overview) for details.
</Tip>

## Baked goods

Baked goods provide higher nutrition than bread and are crafted from ingredients like berries, apples, cheese, eggs, and herbs. Two items — the Minted Cheese Tart and the Duskwheel Skewer — grant potion effects when eaten.

### Special-effect baked goods

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Minted Cheese Tart" icon="sparkles">
    **Nutrition:** 11 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.85

    **Effect:** Regeneration I for 2 seconds (100% chance)

    Combines cheese slices and fresh mint. The highest saturation modifier of any baked good, plus a short burst of regeneration on every bite.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duskwheel Skewer" icon="moon">
    **Nutrition:** 12 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.75

    **Effect:** Night Vision for 15 seconds (100% chance)

    A skewer threaded with chunks of duskwheel cheese. Grants night vision — useful before descending into caves or venturing out at night.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The Duskwheel Skewer requires **Duskwheel Slice**, which comes from a placed Duskwheel cheese wheel block. See [Cheese](/food/cheese) for how to make duskwheel cheese.
</Note>
