Cheese-making is one of the signature crafting systems in Yeast 'n Feast. You use the **Cheese Press** block to produce cheese wheels from milk and rennet, then place the wheel block in the world and right-click it to take individual slices — exactly like cake. Four cheese varieties are available, each with a distinct potion effect when sliced and eaten.

## The Cheese Press

The **Cheese Press** is a functional block crafted from planks and other materials at a crafting table. Place it in your base, then interact with it to open its inventory.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather ingredients">
    You need a **Milk Bottle** and **Rennet** as the core ingredients for every cheese variety. Rennet is crafted from thistle, and the Milk Bottle is filled from a cow. Additional ingredients (herbs, mushrooms, spices) vary by cheese type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load the Cheese Press">
    Place your ingredients into the Cheese Press. The press processes them over time, similar to a furnace or brewing stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect your cheese wheel">
    Once processing is complete, retrieve the cheese wheel item. It stacks to only 1, and placing it in the world creates the cheese wheel block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place and slice">
    Place the cheese wheel block like you would place a cake. Right-click the block to take one slice at a time. Each right-click removes one slice and adds the slice item to your inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Cheese wheel items stack to **1**. Plan your storage accordingly if you are producing cheese in bulk.
</Note>

## Cheese varieties

All cheese slices share **nutrition 3** and **saturation 0.6**. The difference between varieties is entirely in their potion effects. There are two block sizes: **L** (Large, like `LCheeseWheelBlock`) and **M** (Medium, like `MCheeseWheelBlock`), which may affect how many slices a wheel provides.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cheese Wheel" icon="circle">
    **Slice:** Cheese Slice

    **Nutrition:** 3 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.6 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Effect:** None

    The plain cheese wheel. No special effects — just a reliable, low-nutrition snack you can take one slice at a time. Good for replenishing hunger without wasting a full food item.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Freshwheel" icon="leaf">
    **Slice:** Freshwheel Slice

    **Nutrition:** 3 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.6

    **Effect:** Regeneration I for 4 seconds (100% chance)

    A fresh, soft cheese. Each slice grants a short burst of regeneration — useful for topping off health passively while exploring or building.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duskwheel" icon="moon">
    **Slice:** Duskwheel Slice

    **Nutrition:** 3 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.6

    **Effect:** Night Vision for 10 seconds (100% chance)

    An aged, dark-rind cheese. Each slice grants 10 seconds of night vision. Keep a duskwheel placed near your mine entrance and grab a slice before descending.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sharpwheel" icon="sword">
    **Slice:** Sharpwheel Slice

    **Nutrition:** 3 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Saturation:** 0.6

    **Effect:** Strength I for 6 seconds (100% chance)

    A sharp, aged variety. Each slice gives a brief Strength I boost. Taking a slice before a fight adds meaningful burst damage, especially with a sword.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Key ingredients

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Milk Bottle">
    The Milk Bottle is a crafting ingredient for the Cheese Press and returns a glass bottle as a crafting remainder when used in recipes. It stacks to 16. When consumed directly, it removes one random harmful (non-beneficial) effect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rennet">
    Rennet is crafted from thistle — a renewable plant found in the world or grown. Like the Milk Bottle, it returns a glass bottle as a crafting remainder. You will need a steady supply of both for large-scale cheese production.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  The **Duskwheel Skewer** baked good also requires Duskwheel Slice as an ingredient. If you plan to craft skewers, keep a duskwheel block placed so you can take slices on demand. See [Baked Goods](/food/baked-goods) for skewer details.
</Tip>
