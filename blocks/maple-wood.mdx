Maple is a fully implemented wood type added by Yeast 'n Feast. It covers every block and item slot that vanilla wood sets provide — logs, planks, all six structural variants, signs, hanging signs, and boats — giving you a complete building palette with a distinct maple aesthetic. Maple trees generate naturally in Forest biomes and can also be grown from a Maple Sapling.

## Obtaining maple wood

Maple Logs drop from Maple Trees, which spawn in Forest biomes alongside oak and birch trees. You can also grow your own trees by planting a **Maple Sapling** on grass or dirt and waiting for it to mature (or using bone meal to speed growth).

<Tip>
  Use an axe on a Maple Log or Maple Wood block to strip it, converting it to its stripped variant and recovering the bark material.
</Tip>

## Blocks

### Logs and wood

| Block | Notes |
|---|---|
| Maple Log | Raw log with bark on all sides (rotatable pillar) |
| Stripped Maple Log | Log with bark removed; same pillar behaviour |
| Maple Wood | All-bark block (rotatable pillar) |
| Stripped Maple Wood | All-bark block with bark removed |

Strip a Maple Log or Maple Wood block by right-clicking it with any axe. The bark is removed and the block converts to its stripped variant in place.

### Planks and derived blocks

Maple Planks are crafted from any maple log variant. All structural blocks below are crafted from Maple Planks in the same recipes as their oak equivalents.

### Leaves and sapling

| Block | Notes |
|---|---|
| Maple Leaves | Custom `MapleLeavesBlock`; decays when not connected to a log |
| Maple Sapling | Grows into a Maple Tree via `MapleTreeGrower`; can be bonemealed |
| Potted Maple Sapling | Decorative flower-pot variant of the sapling |

### Signs

All maple sign blocks use the custom Maple wood type (`ModWoodTypes.MAPLE`).

| Block | Notes |
|---|---|
| Maple Sign | Floor-standing sign |
| Maple Wall Sign | Wall-mounted sign |
| Maple Hanging Sign | Ceiling-hanging sign |
| Maple Hanging Wall Sign | Wall-mounted hanging sign |
