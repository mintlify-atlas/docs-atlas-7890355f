Yeast 'n Feast adds a range of naturally generating features to vanilla biomes: three new tree species, patches of wild crops and harvestable bushes, and a Tavern structure. Every feature uses Forge's biome modifier system, so generation is consistent and data-pack friendly.

## Trees

All three mod trees spawn at roughly 1-in-25 chunk rarity and require a valid sapling survival position (solid ground, no water). Each can also be grown from its corresponding sapling by planting on grass or dirt and applying bone meal.

### Maple Tree

Maple Trees generate in the **Forest** biome alongside oak and birch. The trunk is built from **Maple Logs** with a fancy spherical foliage shape composed of **Maple Leaves** (`MapleLeavesBlock`). Maple Leaves can be attached to a Tree Tap to collect Maple Syrup.

### Lemon Tree

Lemon Trees generate in **Plains** biomes. The trunk is Oak Log, and the canopy uses a weighted mix of **Lemon Tree Leaves** (common) and **Flowering Lemon Tree Leaves** (rare, ~1-in-9 leaf blocks). The flowering stage uses the `LemonLeavesBlock` custom class and carries an `AGE` property — mature flowering leaves can drop **Lemons**.

### Hawthorn Tree

Hawthorn Trees generate in the **Forest** biome. Like Lemon Trees, the trunk is Oak Log and the canopy mixes **Hawthorn Tree Leaves** (common) with **Flowering Hawthorn Tree Leaves** (rare, ~1-in-9 blocks). Hawthorn uses a cherry-style foliage placer, giving it a wider, drooping canopy shape. Flowering leaves drop **Hawthorn Berries** when broken.

## Wild crop patches

Wild plants generate as small random patches (rarity 1-in-10 chunks) and act as the primary way to obtain ingredients before you establish a farm. Breaking a wild plant drops its corresponding seed or ingredient item.

| Plant | Biome | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| Wild Barley | Plains | Barley Seeds / Barley |
| Wild Garlic | Plains | Garlic |
| Wild Rye | Forest | Rye Seeds / Rye |
| Wild Ginger | Taiga | Ginger |
| Thistle | Forest | Thistle (ingredient) |

<Note>
  **Thistle** is implemented as a `RenewablePlantBlock`, meaning it can regrow over time after being harvested — similar to how sweet berry bushes regrow. A potted variant (`potted_thistle`) is also available for decorating interiors.
</Note>

## Berry bushes

Elderberry Bush and Rose Hips Bush behave identically to the vanilla Sweet Berry Bush: they grow through age stages, deal contact damage at full growth, and drop their berries when broken or right-clicked at max age.

| Bush | Biome | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| Elderberry Bush | Dark Forest | Elderberries |
| Rose Hips Bush | Custom tag: `has_rose_hips` | Rose Hips |

<Tip>
  Both bushes generate at max age (age 3) in the world, so you can harvest them immediately on discovery.
</Tip>

## Structures

Yeast 'n Feast adds the **Tavern** structure to world generation. Taverns house a **Tavern Keeper** villager, who uses a [Keg](/blocks/functional-blocks#keg) as a workstation and offers trades for mod-specific brewed goods. Explore the world to find a Tavern, or check the `data/yeastnfeast/worldgen/structure/tavern.json` configuration for placement details.
