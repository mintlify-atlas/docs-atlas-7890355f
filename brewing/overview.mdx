Brewing is one of the core systems in Yeast 'n Feast. Using the **Keg** block, you combine ingredients, yeast, and a tankard to produce mead — a drinkable item that grants a powerful mob effect when consumed. There are six base mead varieties split across three categories, each tied to a distinct combat or survival buff. Mead stacks to 8 and returns a **Tankard** when drunk.

## The three mead categories

Every mead in Yeast 'n Feast belongs to one of three flavour families. The category determines the mob effect the mead grants when you drink it.

  <Card title="Sweet meads" icon="droplet" href="/brewing/mead-types#sweet-meads">

## How brewing works

All mead is produced inside the **Keg**, a craftable block with a dedicated GUI. You load up to three ingredient slots with the mead's flavour components, place **Yeast** in the yeast slot, and a **Tankard** in the container slot. The Keg processes the recipe over time and deposits finished mead into the output slot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Craft a Keg">
    Build the Keg at a crafting table. This also unlocks the **Keg Stand** advancement. See the [Keg page](/brewing/keg) for the full recipe and slot layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather ingredients">
    Collect the flavour ingredients for the mead you want to brew. Common inputs include honey, molasses, elderberries, hawthorn berries, rose hips, and thornberries. You also need **Yeast** and at least one **Tankard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load the Keg and wait">
    Open the Keg GUI, fill the ingredient and dedicated slots, and wait for the progress bar to complete. Brew time is set per recipe — check JEI for exact durations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect your mead">
    Take the finished mead from the output slot. Each batch fills one tankard, returning an empty Tankard when you drink it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Advancements

Yeast 'n Feast tracks your brewing progress through four advancements. Completing them all requires brewing every mead variety in the mod.

| Advancement | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Keg Stand** | Craft a Keg to start brewing. |
| **Sweet Victory** | Brew all Sweet Meads. |
| **Sour Power** | Brew all Sour Meads. |
| **Floral Finesse** | Brew all Floral Meads. |

<Tip>
  The three category advancements — Sweet Victory, Sour Power, and Floral Finesse — count brewed output, not just item collection. You need to actually produce each mead in a Keg, not receive it through other means.
</Tip>
