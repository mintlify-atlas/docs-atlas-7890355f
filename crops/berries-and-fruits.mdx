Yeast 'n Feast adds two berry bushes, two fruit trees, and two sweet syrups to the game. These ingredients form the backbone of jams, pies, meads, and glazed feast dishes. Unlike farmland crops, berries and fruits are obtained by finding and harvesting world-generated plants rather than planting seeds in tilled soil.

## Berry bushes

Both berry bushes — the **Elderberry Bush** and the **Rose Hips Bush** — work similarly to Minecraft's vanilla Sweet Berry Bush. They grow through four age stages (0–3) and require sufficient sky light (brightness ≥ 9 above the block) to advance.

<Warning>
  Like the Sweet Berry Bush, these bushes slow and damage entities that walk through them. Take care when moving around dense thickets.
</Warning>

**Harvesting:** Right-click a fully mature bush (age 3) to collect berries and reset it to age 1 so it regrows. You cannot pick berries from an immature bush. Bone Meal advances the bush by one growth stage.

### Elderberries

Elderberries drop from the Elderberry Bush at full maturity (2–3 berries per harvest). They are a mild food source on their own and a key ingredient in several processed foods.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | 2 |
| Saturation modifier | 0.1 |

**Uses:** Elderberries Jam, Elderberry Pie, Berry Roll, Oatmeal with Elderberries (Farm and Charm)

### Rose Hips

Rose Hips drop from the Rose Hips Bush at full maturity (2–3 per harvest). They are slightly more satiating than Elderberries raw.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | 2 |
| Saturation modifier | 0.3 |

**Uses:** Rose Hips Jam, Rose Tart

## Fruit trees

### Lemon Tree

The Lemon Tree grows from a **Lemon Sapling** using a custom tree grower. Its leaves cycle through two states: **Flowering Lemon Tree Leaves** and standard **Lemon Tree Leaves**. Lemons drop from the flowering leaf variant.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | 1 |
| Saturation modifier | 0.1 |

**Uses:** Lemon-Glazed Chicken, Lemon Jam

<Tip>
  Lemon Saplings can be placed in a flower pot for decoration.
</Tip>

### Hawthorn Tree

The Hawthorn Tree grows from a **Hawthorn Sapling** and follows the same two-stage leaf cycle: **Flowering Hawthorn Tree Leaves** and standard **Hawthorn Tree Leaves**. Hawthorn Berries are harvested directly from the flowering leaves by right-clicking when the leaf block reaches age 2 (its maximum).

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | 2 |
| Saturation modifier | 0.1 |

**Uses:** Hawthorn Berries Jam, Thornberry Mead

<Steps>
  <Step title="Obtain a sapling">
    Find a naturally generated Hawthorn Tree in the world and break its leaves to collect a Hawthorn Sapling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grow the tree">
    Plant the sapling on grass or dirt and give it time (or Bone Meal) to grow into a full tree. The tree starts with non-flowering leaves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for flowering">
    Over time, the leaf blocks transition from standard Hawthorn Tree Leaves to Flowering Hawthorn Tree Leaves as they reach age 2.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Harvest">
    Right-click flowering leaves at age 2 to collect 2–3 Hawthorn Berries. The leaves reset to age 0 and regrow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Maple Syrup

Maple Syrup is obtained by placing a **Tree Tap** on a Maple Log or Maple Wood block (including stripped variants). The Tree Tap must be placed on a horizontal face of the log. After the configured drip delay elapses, the tap enters a `dripping` state, shown by a particle effect. You can collect Maple Syrup in two ways:

- **Right-click the dripping tap with an empty Glass Bottle** — collects one Maple Syrup bottle directly from the tap and resets it.
- **Place a plain Cauldron on the ground directly below the tap** — when the tap is dripping, it automatically fills the Cauldron, converting it to a Maple Syrup Cauldron. Right-click that cauldron with a Glass Bottle to collect syrup one bottle at a time.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | 6 |
| Saturation modifier | 0.1 |

**Uses:** Maple-Glazed Rabbit, Maple-Rye Pancakes (Farm and Charm), Amber Mead, Blossom Mead

<Note>
  The Tree Tap only attaches to Maple Log and Maple Wood blocks. It will not work on any other log type.
</Note>

The in-game advancement **Maple Story** is earned the first time you collect Maple Syrup from a Tree Tap.

## Molasses

Molasses is a crafting ingredient with the same nutrition profile as Maple Syrup. It is used in breads and meads.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Nutrition | 6 |
| Saturation modifier | 0.1 |

**Uses:** Molasses Bread, Molasses Mead
