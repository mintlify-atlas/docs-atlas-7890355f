Yeast 'n Feast is a Minecraft Forge mod for version 1.20.1 that expands the game's food and farming systems with fermentation, baking, and feasting. Grow barley, rye, ginger, and garlic, forage wild plants from the world, tap maple trees for syrup, brew flavourful meads in a Keg, press cheese wheels with a Cheese Press, preserve fruits as jams in glass jars, and cook hearty feast dishes that grant custom status effects. The mod also introduces a full maple wood set — logs, planks, doors, signs, boats, and more — along with lemon and hawthorn trees that generate naturally.

## What the mod adds

Yeast 'n Feast touches several areas of the game:

- **Crops** — barley, rye, mint, ginger, and garlic farmland crops, plus elderberry and rose hip bushes.
- **Mead brewing** — honey mead, molasses mead, sour mead, thornberry mead, blossom mead, and amber mead, brewed in the Keg and served in a Tankard.
- **Cheese** — four cheese varieties (Cheese Wheel, Freshwheel, Duskwheel, Sharpwheel) produced in the Cheese Press using milk bottles and rennet. Each wheel is placed as a block and sliced like cake.
- **Jams** — eleven jam varieties made with a glass jar and various fruits, including apple, elderberry, lemon, melon, rose hips, and glow berries.
- **Feast dishes** — bowl dishes (sweet porridge, barley and beef stew, salmon chowder, cheese soup) and plate dishes (mead-braised pork, lemon-glazed chicken, maple-glazed rabbit, forager feast, stuffed rye dumplings).
- **Maple wood** — a complete wood set with logs, planks, slabs, stairs, fences, doors, trapdoors, signs, hanging signs, pressure plates, buttons, and boats.
- **World generation** — wild barley, wild rye, wild ginger, and wild garlic generate as flower-type plants in the world. Maple, lemon, and hawthorn trees also spawn naturally.

## Recommended companion mods

<Tip>
  **JEI (Just Enough Items) 15.8.2.26** is strongly recommended. It provides in-game recipe viewing for all of Yeast 'n Feast's crafting stations (including the Keg and Cheese Press), making it much easier to discover and look up recipes without leaving the game.
</Tip>

The mod also provides optional compatibility content for **Farmer's Delight**, **Hexalia**, and **Farm and Charm**. When those mods are detected, additional recipes and items unlock automatically. See the [Mod Compatibility](/compat/overview) section for details.
